\# CardioVision 3D



AI-Driven Cardiovascular Risk Visualization \& Prediction.



\## Objective



CardioVision 3D predicts:



\- Overall Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)

\- LAD stenosis

\- LCX stenosis

\- RCA stenosis



The predictions are visualized on an interactive 3D heart model with explainable AI.
